A free endless runner. A city that never stops.

Neon Dash is a free, single-player endless runner game you play directly in your browser. Switch between three lanes, dodge trains, jump barriers and cargo crates, and slide under glowing gates. Collect coins, catch a shield or magnet, and chase your personal best in a neon city. No download, account, or payment is needed.

How to play Neon Dash

On mobile, swipe left or right to switch lanes, up to jump, and down to slide. On desktop, use the arrow keys or W, A, S, and D; Space also jumps. Dodge trains by changing lanes — they are too tall to jump. Pause with P or the pause button.

Your score is the distance you run plus 20 points per coin. Blue magnets attract coins for 10 seconds, and mint shields block one hit for up to 12 seconds.

Choose your pace

Start with Chill for more room, choose Classic for the original rush, or try Rush for a faster challenge. Speed increases as you travel, up to each mode's limit. Run through Midnight District, Violet Market, and Harbor Run as the city changes every 750 meters.

Use the corners button to leave the expanded game view and change difficulty or runner color. Play in portrait or landscape; supported browsers offer native fullscreen.

Common questions

Can I play on a phone or computer?

Yes. Neon Dash supports touch swipes on phones and tablets, and keyboard controls on computers with a modern browser.

Does Neon Dash work offline?

An internet connection is needed to load the game. You can add it to your Home Screen for a browser-bar-free launch, but offline loading is not supported.

Where is my best score saved?

Your best score, runner color, and sound preference are stored locally in this browser when storage is available. Scores are specific to the browser and device; they do not sync to an account.

Open Neon Dash from your Home Screen to play without browser tabs or the address bar. The game opens in the expanded view automatically.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Keep Open as Web App on if shown, then tap Add.
  3. Launch Neon Dash from its new Home Screen icon.

Android

  1. Open this site in Chrome or Brave.
  2. Open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Launch the new Neon Dash icon.

Device status and gesture indicators may remain visible. The corners button returns to the game settings. An internet connection is needed to load the game.
