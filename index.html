MORE GAME. LESS BROWSER.

Your own arcade.

Open Neon Dash from your Home Screen to play without browser tabs or the address bar. The game opens in the expanded view automatically.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Keep Open as Web App on if shown, then tap Add.
  3. Launch Neon Dash from its new Home Screen icon.

Android

  1. Open this site in Chrome or Brave.
  2. Open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Launch the new Neon Dash icon.

Device status and gesture indicators may remain visible. The corners button returns to the game settings. An internet connection is needed to load the game.
